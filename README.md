## the 3 ideas

* **#01 — `type`:** how much you care about the physical look of someone? 
* **#02 — `not fair`:** why testing on animals for cosmetic products when alternatives exist?
* **#03 — `unhealthy`:** pls stop telling me arguing is the healthy way :O

> **note:** *these are purely my own personal ideas and reflections. i dont mean to judge, accuse, or name-call anyone. i simply wanted to share a few thoughts that bother me in a creative, anonymous space!* :D
